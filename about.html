About

Maple Canada Ramadan Inc.

A Canadian company focused on healthcare, education, innovation, and strategic collaboration.

Our purpose

Maple Canada Ramadan Inc. was established to develop and support initiatives that connect healthcare expertise, professional education, technology, and practical innovation.

The company focuses on building useful, scalable solutions for healthcare professionals, learners, and partner organizations.

Core areas

  • Healthcare and pharmacy innovation
  • Professional and clinical education
  • Digital learning and technology
  • Strategic partnerships and collaboration
  • Publications and educational resources

Our initiatives

Our current initiatives include NeoClinIQ, a visual clinical education platform designed to support pharmacy education, clinical reasoning, and professional development.
